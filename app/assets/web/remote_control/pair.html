Where do I find the token?

  1. Open Spirit on your computer.
  2. Go to Settings → Remote Control.
  3. Copy the pairing token, or scan the QR code shown there to open this page with the token filled in.

The token stays valid until you rotate it in Settings. Pairing stores a cookie on this device for 30 days; you can revoke it from Settings → Remote Control at any time.
